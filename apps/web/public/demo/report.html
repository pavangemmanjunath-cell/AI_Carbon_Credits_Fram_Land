ProgrammeAuthorityScreening statusKey requirements
Green Credit Programme
Government programme
  • Tree plantation is a notified Green Credit activity, but the 2024 tree-plantation methodology targets identified degraded land parcels made available by state forest departments; applicability to private farmland must be confirmed with the administrator (ICFRE).
  • Green Credit Programme is a separate mechanism from CCTS.
 Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) Requires assessment
  • Registration on the Green Credit Programme portal
  • Plantation on land parcels identified/approved under the programme
  • Verification of tree survival per the notified methodology
  • Understanding that Green Credits are not carbon credits and cannot be used for CCTS compliance
MoEFCC — Green Credit Programme (notification and methodology)
ICFRE — Green Credit Programme administrator
Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (offset mechanism)
Compliance market
  • BEE lists agriculture and forestry among sectors covered by accredited carbon verification agencies under the offset mechanism; a project needs an approved methodology, baseline, additionality demonstration, monitoring and third-party verification before any Carbon Credit Certificate is issued.
  • CCTS is a separate mechanism from Green Credit Programme.

  • Approved offset methodology applicable to the activity

  • Registration with the registry under the scheme
