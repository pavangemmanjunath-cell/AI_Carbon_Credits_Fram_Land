## 1. India — Green Credit Programme (GCP)

| Fact | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Legal basis | Green Credit Rules, 2023, notified 12 Oct 2023 under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986, as **S.O. 4458(E)** | [TeamLease RegTech summary](https://teamleaseregtech.com/updates/article/27182/green-credit-rules-2023/); [ICFRE Green Credit Cell](https://english.icfre.gov.in/en/green-credit-cell); [MoEFCC GCP portal](https://www.moefcc-gcp.in/about/aboutGCP) |
| Administrator | Indian Council of Forestry Research and Education (ICFRE), Dehradun, under Rule 7 | [GCP FAQ](https://www.moefcc-gcp.in/faq) |
| Activities in the Rules | Tree plantation, water management, sustainable agriculture, waste management, air-pollution reduction, mangrove conservation/restoration, Ecomark, sustainable building/infrastructure | [SCC Online, 17 Oct 2023](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2023/10/17/green-credit-rules-2023-notified-to-incentivise-environmental-actions-legal-news/) |
| Tree-plantation methodology (first) | Notified **22 Feb 2024, S.O. 884(E)**; plantation blocks "with potential for 1,100 trees per hectare"; one Green Credit per tree after two years | [SCC Online analysis, 11 Feb 2026](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/02/11/ecological-concerns-green-credit-programme-india-analysis/); [Mongabay, Sep 2025](http://india.mongabay.com/2025/09/green-credit-rules-tweaked-to-favour-canopy-cover-remove-trade-provision/) |
| Eligible land | "Degraded forest land parcels under the control and management of the State/UT Forest Department, uploaded on the Green Credit Portal"; each parcel a compact area of **at least 5 ha**, free of encumbrances | [GCP FAQ](https://www.moefcc-gcp.in/faq) |
| Who can register | Any entity, individual, company, PSU or organisation registered on the portal (Government institutions, PSUs, NGOs, private companies, philanthropies, individuals, registered groups) | [GCP FAQ](https://www.moefcc-gcp.in/faq); [MoEFCC GCP About](https://www.moefcc-gcp.in/about/aboutGCP) |
| **Revised methodology (2025)** | Notified **29 Aug 2025, G.S.R. 592(E)**: credits only after a minimum of **five years** of restoration and when **canopy density reaches at least 40 %**; one credit per surviving tree aged five years or more; credits **non-tradable and non-transferable** except between holding company and subsidiaries; may be exchanged once (for compensatory afforestation, CSR, ESG reporting) and are then extinguished | [SCC Online, 11 Feb 2026](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/02/11/ecological-concerns-green-credit-programme-india-analysis/); [Mongabay, Sep 2025](http://india.mongabay.com/2025/09/green-credit-rules-tweaked-to-favour-canopy-cover-remove-trade-provision/); [GCP FAQ](https://www.moefcc-gcp.in/faq) |
| Follow-up guidelines | Office Memorandum FC-11/104/2025 (15 Sep 2025): 10-year maintenance and compensatory-afforestation exchange ratios of 2:1 to 4:1 **(unverified; secondary source)** | [SCC Online, 11 Feb 2026](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/02/11/ecological-concerns-green-credit-programme-india-analysis/) |
| Scale | Parliament answer of 16 Mar 2026: methodology notified 22 Feb 2024 and revised 29 Aug 2025; 4,391 ha registered across 12 states (Gujarat 975 ha) | [PIB PRID 2240640](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2240640&reg=3&lang=1) |
| **GCP ≠ CCTS (official wording)** | "The generation of Green Credit under Green Credit Rules, 2023 is independent of the carbon credit under Carbon Credit Trading Scheme 2023." | [MoEFCC Year-End Review 2023, PIB PRID 1989495](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=1989495&reg=48&lang=2) |
| GCP FAQ on carbon credits | "Carbon Credit focuses only on reducing or removing greenhouse gas emissions (mainly CO₂), while Green Credit covers a broader range of environmental activities"; "The Green Credits issued for tree plantation under GCP shall be non-tradable." | [GCP FAQ](https://www.moefcc-gcp.in/faq) |
| Criticisms | Original 1,100 trees/ha lacked scientific basis; risk of afforesting open natural ecosystems; double-counting concern between Green Credits and CCTS certificates for the same tree | [SCC Online, 11 Feb 2026](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/02/11/ecological-concerns-green-credit-programme-india-analysis/); [Mongabay, Mar 2024](https://india.mongabay.com/2024/03/green-credit-schemes-methodology-doesnt-inspire-confidence-among-experts/) |

**Implication for `config/policies/IN.yaml`:** private farmland is *not* a GCP tree-plantation pathway; the only eligible land is forest-department degraded land ≥5 ha. The screening status for `tree_plantation` and `agroforestry` on a farmer's own plot should be `not_applicable` (with an explanatory reason), not `requires_assessment`. The requirements list must drop "tree survival per the notified methodology" and say: ≥5 years, ≥40 % canopy density, non-tradable, one-time exchange. See §8.
