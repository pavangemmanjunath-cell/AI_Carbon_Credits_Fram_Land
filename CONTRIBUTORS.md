# Contributors

CarbonCreditBuy exists because people give their time to it. This file
recognizes that work. It is maintained by hand, by pull request, under a
simple policy: **real contributions only, no inflation.**

## Policy

1. **A contribution is something that was merged or published.** Code,
   tests, documentation, translations, verified parameter tables, reviewed
   policy rules, design work, reproducible bug reports that led to a fix,
   and domain review of carbon or policy changes all count. Opening an
   account, starring the repository, commenting "+1" or submitting an
   unmerged pull request does not.
2. **One line per person, with what they did.** The entry says which area
   the person worked on so that credit is specific and checkable against
   the git history or the issue tracker. We do not rank contributors or
   count commits.
3. **Nobody is added without their consent.** Contributors may ask to be
   listed under a handle, a full name or not at all. Removal requests are
   honored without discussion.
4. **No organizations here.** Affiliations may be mentioned in a person's
   entry if they wish; organizations that deploy the platform belong in
   [ADOPTERS.md](ADOPTERS.md).
5. **Authorship is separate.** Substantial original components are credited
   in [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md); that list is intentionally short.
6. **AI tools are not contributors.** A change produced with AI assistance
   is credited to the person who reviewed, verified and submitted it, as
   stated in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Sustained contributors (several merged changes over a period of months, or
one large piece of work) are listed here. Smaller one-off fixes are credited
in the release notes for the version that includes them.

## How to add yourself

Open a pull request that adds one line in the relevant section, in the
format `- **Name or handle** — what you contributed (area, link to the
pull request or issue)`. A maintainer confirms the merged work and approves.
