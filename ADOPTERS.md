# Adopters

Organizations and projects that use AgriCarbon Atlas in production, in
research, or in a pilot. Knowing who relies on the platform helps us
prioritize regions, data sources and languages, and helps new users find
peers.

## Rule

**Never add an organization without its permission or verifiable public
evidence.** Acceptable evidence is a public blog post, paper, talk,
procurement notice or repository published by the organization itself that
names AgriCarbon Atlas. A private conversation, a screenshot, or "we heard
that…" is not evidence. When an entry is based on permission rather than a
public source, the pull request that adds it must come from, or be approved
in writing by, someone at that organization, and the entry says so.

Entries are removed on request, without discussion. Listing here is not an
endorsement by the organization of the project, nor by the project of the
organization, and it never implies that any carbon estimate produced with
the platform has been certified.

## How to add an entry

Open a pull request that adds one row to the table below. Keep the
description factual (what you use it for, which regions) and link to the
evidence or state "permission given by <role>, <date>".

### Column guide

- **Type** — farm, cooperative, NGO, research group, university course,
  government agency, consultancy, company.
- **Use** — production (farmers see it), pilot (limited users), research
  (papers, validation), teaching.
- **Regions** — ISO codes (`IN`, `US`, `EU` or member-state codes).
- **Since** — month and year of first use.
- **Evidence / permission** — a URL or a permission note.
